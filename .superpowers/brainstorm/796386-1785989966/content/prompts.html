Brand Brief · 提示词基线
产品:家装前期 AI 设计工作台(给设计师用) · 母语中文
气质:编辑感 · 安静 · 克制 · 有纸质温度 · 偏离 Figma/酷家乐的深色工具风
色板:画布 #faf9f5 · 墨 #1c1a16 · 陶土 #b5532a
字体气质:衬线(Fraunces / 思源宋体)+ 几何无衬线 · 拒绝任何渐变 / 阴影 / 玻璃拟态
参考气质:Aesop · Pentagram wordmarks · NYT Opinion · Heath Ceramics · The Yale Architectural Journal
要避开的:卡通插画 · 渐变色 · 3D · 极简单线条几何 · "科技感"未来主义
现状:陶土描边方框 + 「砌」字单字印章,被你自己评「太平庸」
Prompt A · 编辑感衬线字标(纯字母路线)
Minimalist editorial logo mark for a home-design brand. A single uppercase letter Q rendered in a contemporary high-contrast serif typeface (Fraunces or Tiempos style). The Q has a confident, subtle tilt and a small terracotta-orange (#b5532a) accent stroke inside the counter. Cream paper background (#faf9f5). Flat vector quality, no shadows, no gradients. Inspired by Bloomberg Businessweek, Aesop, Pentagram wordmarks. Single mark, square composition.

注释:MJ 加 --ar 1:1 --style raw --s 50 · GPT/Gemini 直接粘贴

Prompt B · 墨痕建筑符号(空间隐喻)
Minimalist logo mark for a sophisticated home-design AI tool. A single confident ink stroke that reads as an open doorway or window arch, combined with one small solid square. Drawn with the warmth of a brush on warm cream paper (#faf9f5). Two colors only: deep ink-black (#1c1a16) for the stroke, terracotta-orange (#b5532a) for the square. Editorial, tactile, distinctive — like a museum imprint or architecture monograph mark. Flat, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D. Single mark, square format.

注释:这个方向最接近"纸质工作室"气质,墨痕是首选语言

Prompt C · 不对称几何(动态编辑)
Logo mark for an AI-powered home design workspace. An asymmetric geometric symbol — three rectangular blocks of varying widths arranged in a confident, dynamic composition (deliberately not a simple stack, not a grid). Think NYT Opinion section mark or a contemporary art museum identity. Color: deep charcoal-black (#1c1a16) for two blocks, terracotta-orange (#b5532a) for one. Cream paper background (#faf9f5). Flat vector quality, no gradients, no shadows. Editorial, sophisticated, immediately recognizable at favicon scale. Square format.

注释:和第一屏的"三叠"不同 — 这次要"打破堆叠的秩序感"

Prompt D · 砌字重制(中文锚点 + 解构)
Logo mark that reimagines the Chinese character 砌 (literally "to stack bricks" — composed of three 土 radicals stacked vertically). Show the character redrawn as a custom, hand-drawn logogram with confident brush energy and intentional imperfection, in deep ink-black on warm cream paper (#faf9f5). One stroke should feel slightly off-grid or disrupted, suggesting iteration / work-in-progress / the AI making drafts. A single small terracotta-orange (#b5532a) mark (dot or short stroke) as accent. Editorial, tactile, distinctive — like an architect's monogram. Square format.

注释:这条最容易中文出图失败 — 如果生图模型不识"砌"字,改成 "Chinese character meaning stack bricks, three 土 radicals stacked"

建议跑法:4 个 prompt 各跑 4-8 张,挑你心动的 2-3 张回来;或者你自己微调 prompt 加约束(比如"加入 wordmark"、"出 favicon 版本"——这些都可以二轮做)。
